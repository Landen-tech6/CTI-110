# How to Make a Sandwich

## Assumptions

- The robot is standing at a counter.
- The bread is in a bag on the counter.
- The sandwich ingredients are on the counter.
- A clean plate is on the counter.
- The robots hands are empty.

## Steps

1. Pick up the bag of bread.
2. Open the bag of bread.
3. Remove two slices of bread from the bag.
4. Place one slice of bread on the plate.
5. Pick up the mayonnaise.
6. Open the mayonnaise container.
7. Pick up a knife.
8. Spread mayonnaise across the top of the bread.
9. Put the knife down.
10. Pick up one slice of turkey.
11. Place the turkey on the bread.
12. Pick up one slice of cheese.
13. Place the cheese on the turkey.
14. Pick up one piece of lettuce.
15. Place the lettuce on the cheese.
16. Pick up one slice of tomato.
17. Place the tomato on the lettuce.
18. Pick up the second slice of bread.
19. Place the second slice of bread on the tomato.

## Done When

The sandwich is assembled on the plate with the second slice of bread on top
